## Conflicts to resolve

Seven facts need the client's answer before launch, because the new site will be treated as the authority.

- [ ] **Weekend hours.** 8 AM to 6 PM (Waze, BrewAtlas) or 8 AM to 4 PM (Yelp, old site page)? Square accepts Saturday orders until 6:59 PM, which favours 6 PM.
- [ ] **Auburn Avenue.** Is the site at 364 Auburn Ave NE still operating, and under what name and hours?
- [ ] **The Black Coffee Company.** What is the relationship, if any, to the Lakewood Heights business of the same name?
- [ ] **Ownership wording.** Black-owned and LGBTQ-friendly are stated. Are women-owned and LGBTQ-owned accurate?
- [ ] **Evening service.** Does the cocktail and dessert menu still run, and on which nights?
- [ ] **Open mic and events.** Is there a current recurring schedule?
- [ ] **Canonical name.** Black Coffee ATL for the brand and Black Coffee ATL - Westside for this location?
